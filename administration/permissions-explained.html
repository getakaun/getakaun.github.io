Permissions explained

A permission lets a user do one thing in one area of Akaun. Read this page before you make a group, give a user extra permissions or make a token. To change the permissions, see Users and groups.

Areas and abilities​

Each area of Akaun has a list of abilities. In Users & Groups and on the New access token page, each area shows as a row. You choose a level of access for the row, or you switch on single abilities. See Levels of access.

  • View opens the screen of the area and shows its items. Without View, the screen does not open, and its item does not show in the sidebar.
  • Add, Change and Delete make, change and remove the items of the area.
  • Some areas have more abilities, for example Send and Void on Invoices. Each one lets a user do one special task.

A superuser has all the abilities in all the areas. The levels and the abilities do not apply to a superuser.

Levels of access​

For each area, you select one level: None, View, Edit, Full or Custom. A level is a quick way to choose a set of abilities. Akaun keeps each ability on its own. A sentence under the name of the area tells what the choice allows, for example "Can see, add, change and send invoices. Cannot delete or void."

  • None gives no ability in the area.
  • View gives the View ability only.
  • Edit gives View and each ability that adds or changes items.
  • Full gives all the abilities of the area, except Adjustments.
  • Custom shows a switch for each ability of the area, with its description. Switch on the abilities that you need.

Read these notes:

  • A set of abilities that is not the same as a level shows as Custom. For example, the Data Entry group has Add on Records but no View. One ability never gives a different ability.
  • Adjustments on Records is never part of a level. It always has its own switch under Records. Thus you give it only on purpose.
  • An ability with a warning sign gives a lot of power. Examples are Adjustments on Records and Manage on Settings. Give them only on purpose.
  • A level that includes an ability that you cannot choose is not available. Hold the pointer on it to see why.

Tasks that need more than one ability​

  • To mark an invoice as sent, a user needs Send on Invoices and Add on Records.
  • To cancel a sent invoice, a user needs Void on Invoices and Change on Records. To make a new invoice in its place, the user also needs Add on Invoices.
  • To make an invoice from a quotation, a user needs Convert on Quotations and Add on Invoices.
  • To revise a quotation, a user needs Revise and Add on Quotations.
  • To merge two contacts, a user needs Merge, Change and Delete on Contacts.
  • To record a statement line as a transfer, a user needs Add on Reconciliation and Add on Records.
  • To read a file again on Auto Import, a user needs Upload and Change on Auto Import.
  • To record a payment from an invoice page, a user needs View on Invoices and Add on Records.

To confirm the items of a file on Auto Import, a user needs only Confirm on Auto Import. The user does not need a permission on Records.

Records​

Records is one permission area for all kinds of record. It covers expenses, income, transfers, payments, opening balances and journal entries. A user cannot have permission for expenses only.

Reports​

Reports is view only. A report shows figures and never changes them. Thus Reports has only the View ability.

Adjustments​

Adjustments is an ability in the Records area. It lets a user save a record between any two accounts. Akaun decides from the accounts if a record needs this ability. These records are everyday records, and they do not need Adjustments:

  • An expense: money out of a money account into an expense category.
  • A bill to pay later: money out of Accounts Payable into an expense category.
  • An income: money out of an income category into a money account.
  • A transfer: money from one money account to a different money account.
  • A payment: money from a money account to Accounts Payable, or from Accounts Receivable into a money account.
  • An opening balance.
  • A split: one account that holds or owes money, and two or more categories of the same kind.

All other records need Adjustments. Examples are a correction between two categories, a record with two money accounts and a category, and a record with partner capital. On the record form, Adjustments also gives the user a choice of all accounts.

caution

No group that Akaun makes gives Adjustments. No level includes Adjustments, also not Full on Records. A user with this ability can make the books show any figures, and the books still add up. Give it only to a person that you trust with the books. Give it on purpose, to one user, as an extra permission.

See How a record moves money.

Quotations and invoices​

Send and Void on Invoices change the books: they add or remove an Invoice record. Thus each one also needs a permission on Records. See Tasks that need more than one ability.

The groups that Akaun makes have permissions on Quotations and Invoices, as the table in Users and groups shows. A new group that you make has none. Choose a level for them in the group.

Settings​

Settings has two abilities:

  • View lets a user open Settings and see all the tabs. Akaun never shows the key of an AI provider.
  • Manage lets a user change the settings. Examples are the company details, the templates of printed documents, the number format, the AI providers and Rebuild search index.

A user with View and no Manage sees the settings as read only. A note at the top of the page tells the user to ask an administrator for Settings › Manage.

Two parts also have a check of a different area:

  • The Books tab shows only for a user with View on Reports.
  • To choose a default account, a user needs Change on Accounts.

The groups that Akaun makes have View on Settings only. Thus at the start, only a superuser can change the settings. To let a different user change them, give Manage on Settings.

See Settings reference.

Users & Groups​

Users & Groups has one ability, Manage. Only a superuser group has it. You cannot give it to a different group, to one user, to a token or to an app. Thus only a superuser can open Users & Groups.

What tokens and apps can do​

A user can let a program act for them, with a personal access token or with a connected app. See Your profile and Connect AI assistants.

Each group has a second list of areas: Members may hand on to tokens & apps. It is the most that the tokens and the apps of a member can do. A token or an app can do an action only when all of these are true:

  • The token or the app has the permission.
  • One of the groups of the user lets the user hand it on.
  • The user can do the action at that time.

A permission that a user has as an extra permission is never handed on. See What members may hand on.

How the old permissions changed​

Before this version, each area had the same four actions. Akaun changed the permissions of each group at the upgrade. In the areas of the book, no user can do more or less than before.

Old permissionNew abilities
Change on Auto ImportChange, Confirm and Profiles
Delete on ContactsDelete and Merge
Change on QuotationsChange, Revise and Convert
Change on InvoicesChange, Send and Void
Change on ReconciliationChange and Match
The Adjustments areaAdjustments on Records

Settings is different. Before, all users could change the settings. Now each group got View on Settings, and no Manage. Thus only a superuser can change the settings until you give Manage to a group or a user.

Each group can hand on to tokens and apps all the permissions that it gives, except Adjustments and the Settings abilities. All the old API tokens stopped working. See Your profile.

Notes and limits​

  • A change to the permissions applies on the next action of the user, and of each token and app of the user.
  • A user who is in no group has no permissions, and extra permissions have no effect. At the next start of the server, Akaun puts that user into Administrators. See Stop the access of a user.
  • An AI assistant that connects as a user can read only the areas that the user can view. See Connect AI assistants.
