Settings reference

This page tells what each setting in Settings does, tab by tab. To prepare a new book in the correct order, use Prepare your book.

To open the settings, click Settings in the sidebar. The tabs are General, Company, Ledger, Intelligence, Templates and Advanced.

info

To open Settings, you need the View permission on Settings. To change a setting, you need the Manage permission on Settings. Without Manage, Akaun shows all the tabs as read only, with a note at the top. The groups that Akaun makes give View only. Thus at the start, only a superuser can change the settings. Akaun never shows the key of an AI provider. See Permissions explained.

Save your changes​

Each part of a tab has its own Save button. On the Ledger tab, the button is Save defaults. Click the button of the part that you changed before you go to a different part. After a save, Akaun shows Settings saved.

If you have unsaved changes and you click a different tab or a different screen, Akaun shows Unsaved changes:

  • Click Keep editing to stay and save your changes.
  • Click Discard changes to remove your changes and continue.

A reload of the page, or a closed browser tab, removes unsaved changes without a message. The Unsaved changes message also does not cover the Default accounts lists on the Ledger tab.

General​

The General tab has two parts: Display and Numbering.

Display​

  • Currency: the main currency of the book. All amounts show in this currency. Akaun changes the amounts of foreign records into it.
  • Default payment account: the account that new expenses and income use first. You can still change the account on each record. This row shows only when the book has two or more cash or bank accounts. At this time, a change in this row is not kept. To change this account, use Default transaction account on the Ledger tab.
  • Chart of Accounts: click Open accounts → to open the Accounts screen.

Click Save under these rows to save Currency.

caution

Currency locks when the first record, quotation or invoice exists. An opening balance is also a record. After the lock, Currency shows a lock icon and you cannot change it.

See Foreign currency.

Numbering​

Numbering sets one number format for expenses, income, payments, quotations and invoices. Click a token, or drag it, to add it to the format: PREFIX, YYYY, YY, MM, DD and SEQ. Type other characters, for example a dash, directly in the format. The Preview line shows an example number. Click Save under Numbering.

Prepare your book tells what each token means.

The help text lists the codes EX, IN, CL, QT and IV, but it does not tell what CL is. CL is the code for a payment.

caution

The number format locks when the first record, quotation or invoice exists. After the lock, the tokens and the Save button do not show, and you cannot change the format.

Company​

Akaun shows these details on printed quotations and invoices.

  • Logo: click Upload to add a JPEG or PNG image of 5 MB or smaller. Click Replace to use a different image, or Remove to remove it. At this time, the logo does not print on quotations or invoices.
  • Letterhead: a JPEG or PNG image of 5 MB or smaller, usually a wide banner with your logo, name and address. It prints at the top of quotations and invoices, in place of the company name, when Header on the Templates tab is Letterhead. Upload, Replace and Remove work as for the logo.
  • Company Name: the name of the business.
  • Address: the address of the business.
  • Registration No.: the registration number of the business.

Click Save to save the fields.

Ledger​

info

The Ledger tab shows only for a user with the View permission on Reports.

Default accounts​

Akaun uses these accounts for the automatic line of some records. Each list shows only the accounts that fit the default.

DefaultWhat Akaun uses it for
Accounts receivableAn invoice that you send, and a payment from a customer.
Accounts payableThe amounts that the business must pay to suppliers and other parties, for example a bill that you pay later.
Opening balancesThe other line of each opening balance.
Sales revenueAn invoice that does not choose a different revenue account.
Uncategorised expenseAn imported expense with no matching expense account.
Default transaction accountThe account that new expenses and income use first. Default payment account on the General tab shows this account.
Uncategorised incomeAn imported income with no matching revenue account.

Click Save defaults to save. Akaun shows Default accounts updated. To change a default, you need the Change permission on Accounts and the Manage permission on Settings. Without them, the lists and the button are not available.

caution

Do not change Accounts receivable after you send invoices. A payment from a customer then cannot settle an invoice that you sent before the change.

See Prepare your book.

Ledger integrity check​

The check adds the lines of each record and makes sure that each record balances. It only reads the book. It changes nothing.

  1. On the Ledger tab, find Ledger integrity check.
  2. Click Check now.

If all records balance, Akaun shows The ledger is in balance. All records and the trial balance agree.

If a record does not balance, Akaun shows the number of unbalanced records, for example 2 unbalanced records. Each line shows the record number and the difference, for example Record #41 is out of balance by 10.00. If the totals of all accounts also do not agree, Akaun shows Trial balance difference and the amount. Report this problem. See Troubleshooting.

Migration results​

Migration results shows only after Akaun converted a book from an older version. It shows only when the conversion had to make decisions without you. Each part lists the records that the conversion changed, for example Uncategorised records or Files that could not be found. Check each record in the list.

Akaun keeps the previous files in a pre-chart- folder next to the database. See Backups and upgrades.

Intelligence​

The Intelligence tab controls Auto Import. It has three parts. Providers holds the AI providers that read your documents. Import profiles holds the saved ways to read one kind of document. Processing controls how Akaun processes the files. Click Save at the bottom of the tab to save the changes on this tab. Import profiles shows only for a user with the View permission on Auto Import.

See Connect an AI provider and Import profiles.

Templates​

The Templates tab controls printed quotations and invoices, and the terms that new ones start with.

Under Printed documents:

  • Layout: shows Standard. At this time, Akaun has one layout only, and you cannot change it.
  • Accent color: the color of the printed documents. Choose a preset color, or choose a different color.
  • Header: what prints at the top of the page. Company name prints the name as text. Letterhead prints the letterhead image from the Company tab in its place. The layout decides where the header goes. You can choose Letterhead only after you upload a letterhead. If you later remove the letterhead, the documents print the company name again.

Under Default terms:

  • Invoice due in (days): a new invoice is due this number of days after its issue date. Type 0 for due on receipt. Leave it empty for no due date.
  • Quotation valid for (days): a new quotation expires this number of days after its issue date. Leave it empty for no expiry date.

Type a whole number from 0 to 365. The default for each setting is 30. A change applies only to new invoices and quotations. The due date of an invoice that you make from a quotation also comes from Invoice due in (days).

Click Save to save the color and the terms.

Advanced​

The Advanced tab has Search index. The search index is the text that the search box on each list uses.

  1. On the Advanced tab, find Rebuild search index.
  2. Click Rebuild.

Akaun reads the stored files again. Then it makes the search text again for all records, quotations, invoices and contacts. The row shows the progress. At the end, it shows the number of records. Use this when the search does not find a record that it must find.

Notes and limits​

  • Each setting applies to the full book and to all users.
  • Your name, your password and the order of your screens are on your profile, not in Settings. See Your profile.
