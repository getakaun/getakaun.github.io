Your profile

Your profile holds the settings that apply only to you. Examples are your name, your password and the order of your screens. All users can open their own profile. No permission is necessary.

Open your profile​

  1. At the bottom of the sidebar, click the user menu button (three dots) next to your name.
  2. Click Profile.

The My Profile screen opens. It has five tabs: Profile, Security, Access tokens, Connected apps and Navigation.

Profile​

caution

If you are the admin user, do not change the username. At each start, Akaun adds a new superuser with the username admin and the password akaun-admin when no user has the username admin. See Users and groups.

  1. Click the Profile tab.
  2. Change Display name, Email or Username.
  3. Click Save changes.

Akaun shows Profile updated.

Email and Username are necessary. Each user must have a different email and a different username. If the step fails, Akaun shows one of these messages:

  • Email or username is already taken.
  • Enter a valid email address.

Security​

Use this tab to change your password.

  1. Click the Security tab.
  2. In Current password, type your password.
  3. In New password, type a new password. Use 8 characters or more.
  4. In Confirm new password, type the new password again.
  5. Click Change password.

Akaun shows Password changed. Akaun also disconnects all your connected apps and revokes all your access tokens. The other browsers where you are signed in stay signed in.

If the step fails, Akaun shows one of these messages:

  • Current password is incorrect.
  • New password must be at least 8 characters.
  • Passwords do not match.

Access tokens​

A personal access token lets a program, for example a script or an AI assistant, connect to Akaun as you. Each token has its own name, expiry date and permissions. Only you can make a token for yourself. The program sends the token in the header Authorization: Bearer akp_…. A token works on the API of Akaun and on MCP. See Connect AI assistants.

caution

A program with your token can do all the actions that the token permits, also changes and deletes if you select them. Give each token only the permissions that the program needs. Give the token only to a program that you trust.

See your tokens​

Click the Access tokens tab. The tab shows one row for each token. You can also open the tab with the address /profile?tab=token.

Each row shows:

  • the name of the token,
  • akp_… and the last four characters of the token,
  • what the token can do in each area, for example Invoices Edit or Records View + Adjustments. Hold the pointer on an area to see a sentence that tells what the token can do there,
  • Expires: the date when the token stops working, Never, or an Expired badge,
  • Last used: the date of the last use, or Never,
  • the Regenerate and Revoke buttons.

If you have no tokens, the tab shows No access tokens yet.

Make a token​

  1. Click the Access tokens tab.
  2. Click New token. The New access token page opens.
  3. Under Token, in Name, type a name that tells what the token is for, for example Claude — bookkeeping. Each token must have a different name.
  4. In Expires, select the last day when the token works. See Choose the expiry date.
  5. Under Permissions, select a level for each area that the program needs. See Choose the permissions of a token.
  6. Read the This token can panel at the side. It tells, for each area, what the token can do.
  7. At the bottom of the page, click Create token.
  8. Akaun shows the token one time, with the warning Copy the token now. It won't be shown again. Click Copy.
  9. Keep the token in a safe place, for example a password manager.
  10. Click Done. Akaun shows the list of your tokens again.

The token starts with akp_. After you click Done, Akaun shows only the last four characters of the token.

The bar at the bottom of the page shows after your first change. To remove your changes, click Discard.

Choose the expiry date​

Expires shows a date 6 months from today. Click the field to select a different date. You can select only a date after today. The token works until the end of that day (UTC).

To make a token that never expires, click × in the field. The field then shows Never expires, and a warning says "Anyone who gets this token can use it until you revoke it." To set a date again, click the field and select a date.

caution

A token that never expires works until you revoke it. Use an expiry date when you can.

Choose the permissions of a token​

Permissions shows one row for each area, for example Records or Invoices. For each area, select None, View, Edit, Full or Custom. A sentence under the name of the area tells what your choice lets the token do. See Levels of access.

  • Permissions offers only what you may hand on to tokens and apps. Your administrator sets this on your groups. See What members may hand on.
  • An area that you cannot hand on shows You can't hand this on.
  • A level that includes an ability that you cannot hand on is not available. Hold the pointer on it to see why.
  • Adjustments on Records is not part of a level. To give it, switch it on by its name.

If nothing can be handed on, Permissions says that an administrator chooses what each group may hand on, on Users & Groups. Then ask your administrator.

What a token can do​

A token never does more than both of these:

  • the permissions that you selected for the token,
  • what you may hand on to tokens and apps at the time of each request.

Thus when your administrator removes a permission from your group, or from what your group may hand on, each of your tokens loses it immediately. The permissions that you have as extra permissions are never handed on to a token.

Replace or remove a token​

  • To replace the secret of a token, click Regenerate, and then click Regenerate again in the message. The new token shows above the list one time only. Click Copy, and then click Done. The old token stops working immediately. The name, the permissions and the expiry date of the token stay the same.
  • To remove a token, click Revoke, and then click Revoke again in the message.
  • To remove all your tokens, click Revoke all N tokens under the list, and then click Revoke all in the message. The link shows when you have two or more tokens.

An expired token has no Regenerate button. You cannot regenerate it. Make a new token instead.

A program that uses a revoked token cannot connect any more. You cannot undo a revoke. You can have 50 tokens or fewer. A new password revokes all your tokens.

A superuser can also see and revoke your tokens in Users & Groups, but cannot make a token for you. See Revoke the tokens of a user.

Connected apps​

The Connected apps tab shows each app that you let read your data, for example an AI assistant. For each app, it shows:

  • the name of the app,
  • the access that you gave, for example records:read,
  • the date of the connection and the date when it expires,
  • Last used: the last time that the app read your data, or Never.

If no app is connected, the tab shows No apps connected.

To disconnect an app:

  1. Click the Connected apps tab.
  2. Find the app.
  3. Click Revoke access.

Akaun shows App access revoked. The app cannot read your data any more. To use the app again, connect it again.

A new password also disconnects all your apps. Sign out does not disconnect them.

A connected app also never does more than what you may hand on to tokens and apps at that time.

Navigation​

Use this tab to change the order of the main screens in the sidebar. You can also choose the screens on the bottom bar of a phone. The list shows only the screens that you have the View permission for.

  1. Click the Navigation tab.
  2. Drag an item by its handle to change the order.
  3. Select Show on mobile for each screen that you want on the bottom bar.
  4. Click Save changes.

Akaun shows Navigation order saved.

You can select Show on mobile for five screens or fewer. After the fifth, the other boxes are not available.

See Choose your navigation.

Notes and limits​

  • The Connected apps tab is empty when the administrator did not enable OAuth. See Connect AI assistants.
  • Your groups and permissions are not on your profile. Only a superuser can change them. See Users and groups.
