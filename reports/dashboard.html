Dashboard

The Dashboard shows how the business performs. It shows the headline figures, the trends of the last 12 months, the receivables and payables, the sales pipeline and the tasks to do. Use it for a fast check. For the full figures, open the reports.

Each figure comes from the same calculation as its report. When you open the report from a figure, the report shows the same number.

info

You need the View permission on Dashboard. Without it, the Dashboard item does not show in the sidebar, and Akaun opens Settings instead. Of the groups that Akaun creates, only Administrators has this permission. See Permissions explained.

Each section also needs the permission of its source screen. See Who sees which section.

Open the Dashboard​

  1. Click Dashboard.

The top of the screen shows Welcome back and your username.

If the book has no records, the Dashboard shows No records yet and no figures. Click Add a record or Import a document to start.

Choose the period​

The period bar is at the top of the screen. It has four periods:

PeriodDates
This monthFrom the first day of this month to today.
This quarterFrom the first day of this quarter to today.
Year to dateFrom 1 January to today.
Last 12 monthsFrom the first day of the month 11 months ago to today.

Every period ends today. At the start, the Dashboard shows This month.

Choose the comparison period​

Each headline figure shows its change against a comparison period. Next to compared with, choose one of these:

  • Previous period: the same number of days at the start of the previous month or quarter. For example, 1–16 October compares with 1–16 September.
  • Same period last year: the same dates one year earlier.

Year to date and Last 12 months always compare with the same period last year. For these periods, the two choices give the same dates.

The dates of the comparison period show in brackets after the selector.

The period and the comparison are in the address of the page. If you send the link to a colleague, the colleague sees the same period.

The four headline figures​

Each figure shows these items:

  • A change chip: the change against the comparison period, as a percentage. Green shows good news. Red shows bad news. The chip shows new when the comparison period has no value.
  • A small chart (a sparkline) of the last 12 months.

Click a figure to open its report with the same dates.

Revenue​

Revenue is the total income for the period. It opens Profit & Loss.

Net profit​

Net profit is the revenue minus the expenses for the period. If the expenses are more than the revenue, the label changes to Net loss and the figure shows in red.

Below the figure, Akaun shows the Net margin: the net profit as a percentage of the revenue. The margin of the comparison period shows after it, for example (was 12%). If the period has no revenue, the line shows No revenue in this period.

It opens Profit & Loss.

Cash balance​

Cash balance is the total of the cash and cash equivalents today. These are the accounts with the sub-type Cash, Bank, Wallet or Prepaid/debit card. The change chip compares the balance today with the balance at the end of the comparison period.

When the cash falls, the figure also shows the Runway. The runway is the number of months that the cash lasts at the recent rate:

Runway = cash balance today ÷ average fall in cash for each month

Akaun calculates the average fall over the last three complete months. The runway shows only when the cash balance is more than zero and the cash fell over these three months. If the runway is less than six months, it shows in amber.

It opens Cash Flow Statement.

Receivables​

Receivables is the total that customers owe you today. Below the figure, Akaun shows the overdue part in red. The bar shows the ageing of the total: Not yet due, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days and Over 90 days overdue.

This figure has no change chip and no sparkline. Akaun keeps no dated history of the ageing.

It opens the Contacts screen. If you do not have the View permission on Contacts, it opens the Records screen with the unpaid records. See Contacts.

Revenue and expenses​

This chart shows the revenue and the expenses for each of the last 12 months, as bars. A line shows the net profit of each month. The months before the selected period show lighter.

Click a month to open the Profit & Loss for that month.

Cash balance and Balance sheet​

Cash balance​

This chart shows the cash and cash equivalents at the end of each of the last 12 months. The last point is today. Click Cash flow to open the Cash Flow Statement for the period.

Balance sheet​

This card shows the Assets, the Liabilities and the Equity today. The assets are always equal to the liabilities plus the equity. Click Open report to open the Balance Sheet for today.

The Balance sheet card does not change with the period. It always shows today.

If the balance sheet does not balance​

Sometimes the card shows The balance sheet does not balance and the difference. This tells you that a record in the book is not correct. The lines of each record must add to zero. This message shows only when the data is damaged. Do not use the figures until you correct the problem.

  1. Click Settings.
  2. Click the Ledger tab.
  3. In Ledger integrity check, click Check now.
  4. Read the result.

The Ledger tab shows only if you have the View permission on Reports. The check only reads the book. It changes nothing. If a record is wrong, the result gives its record number and the difference. Give this result to your administrator.

Net profit to cash flow​

Profit and cash are not the same. This panel shows, step by step, how the net profit of the period becomes the change in cash. Each figure comes from the Cash Flow Statement and Profit & Loss of the period.

StepWhat it shows
Net profitRevenue minus expenses.
Working capital changesChanges in receivables, payables and inventory. For example, a sale that the customer has not paid increases the profit, but not the cash.
Operating cash flowCash from operating activities. This is the net profit plus the working capital changes.
Capital expenditurePurchases of equipment and other fixed assets.
Free cash flowOperating cash flow minus capital expenditure.
Financing activitiesLoans, owner contributions and drawings.
Unclassified accountsCash movements on accounts that have no sub-type. This step shows only when there are such movements.
Net change in cashClosing cash minus opening cash.

Example: you buy a machine​

You buy a machine for 10,000.00 and pay from the bank:

  • The bank balance falls by 10,000.00. Capital expenditure shows −10,000.00, and the Net change in cash falls by the same amount.
  • The machine is an asset, not an expense. The Net profit does not change.

Akaun does not calculate depreciation. To put the cost of the machine into the profit over its life, record the depreciation with a journal entry. A journal entry needs the Adjustments permission. See How a record moves money.

If the panel shows The cash flow statement does not reconcile, open the Cash Flow Statement to find the cause.

Top expenses and Top revenue​

These two lists show the largest expense accounts and revenue accounts for the period. Each list shows the six largest accounts. The other accounts are added together in one Other line. Thus the list adds up to the total of the Profit & Loss.

Each line shows these items:

  • The amount.
  • The percentage of the total.
  • A change chip against the comparison period. For an expense, a fall shows green.

Click a line to open the Records screen. It shows the records of that account for the same dates. The Other line does not open. Click Open report to open the Profit & Loss.

Receivables and Payables​

These two panels show the amounts that are outstanding today:

  • Receivables: the amounts that customers owe you.
  • Payables: the amounts that you owe to suppliers.

Each panel shows these items:

  • The outstanding balance.
  • The overdue amount and the number of overdue items.
  • The ageing bar: Not yet due, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days and Over 90 days.
  • The three contacts with the largest balances.

Click a contact to open its page. Click Contacts to open the Contacts screen. These links show only if you have the View permission on Contacts. The figures are the same as on the Contacts screen. See Contacts and balances.

Sales pipeline​

This panel shows the sales that are not complete:

FigureWhat it shows
Open quotationsThe value and number of open quotations. The note shows how many expire this week.
Accepted quotationsAccepted quotations that you did not convert to an invoice yet.
Invoiced salesThe value and number of invoices sent in the period.
Outstanding invoicesInvoices that customers did not pay completely. The note shows how many are overdue, and their value.

Click a figure to open the Quotations or Invoices screen. See Quotations and Invoices.

Action items​

This list shows the tasks that keep the books correct. Click an item to open the screen where you do the task.

ItemOpens
Balance sheet does not balanceThe Balance Sheet for today.
Imported documents to reviewAuto Import. See Review and confirm.
Overdue invoicesInvoices.
Unpaid billsRecords, with the unpaid expenses. See Bills you pay later.
Unreconciled bank linesThe reconciliation screen of the account. If more than one account has such lines, it opens Accounts. See Bank reconciliation.
Accounts without a sub-typeAccounts. Give each account a sub-type, so that the reports classify it. See Accounts and categories.

An item shows only when it has something to do. If no item shows, the list shows No open items. The books are up to date.

Recent transactions​

This list shows the eight newest records of all types: expenses, income, payments, transfers, opening balances, invoices and journal entries. Each row shows the description, the record type, the contact and the date.

  • A plus sign shows that cash came in.
  • A minus sign shows that cash went out.
  • No sign shows that the cash did not change, for example a transfer between two of your own accounts.

Click a row to open the record. Click View all to open the Records screen.

Who sees which section​

You need the View permission on Dashboard to open the page. Each section also needs the View permission of the screen that its figures come from:

SectionPermission
Revenue, Net profit and Cash balance figures, Revenue and expenses, Cash balance chart, Balance sheet, Net profit to cash flow, Top expenses, Top revenueView on Reports
Receivables figure, Receivables and Payables panels, Recent transactionsView on Records
Open quotations and Accepted quotationsView on Quotations
Invoiced sales and Outstanding invoicesView on Invoices
Each action itemView on the screen that the item opens

If you do not have a permission, Akaun does not show the section. It does not show the section empty.

Notes​

  • The Dashboard updates by itself when somebody changes a record, a payment, an account, an invoice, a quotation or an import.
  • "Today" is the date on the server.
  • The Dashboard has no export. Use the reports to export the figures.
